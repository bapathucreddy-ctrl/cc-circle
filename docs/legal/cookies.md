# Cookie Policy

CC Creative Labs · Updated 17 October 2026

Cookies, browser storage and equivalent storage in the mobile apps.

## Essential sign-in storage

The CC Circle website uses session storage for its authentication session, separated between customer and professional apps. This is needed to keep you signed in within that tab and is cleared when you sign out or the browser ends the tab’s session. Mobile apps use operating-system secure storage instead; a mobile sign-in can persist until sign-out. These storage mechanisms are not advertising cookies.

## Appearance preferences

The website stores the selected light or dark appearance in local storage under cc-circle-mode. It remains until changed or browser site data is cleared. Native apps store the same preference on the device. Blocking storage may reset your preference and can prevent sign-in from persisting.

## Third-party services

Google sign-in, Mapbox maps, Cashfree checkout and connected or embedded media may use their own cookies, storage or network identifiers when you use those features. CC Circle does not control provider storage on their own sites. Embedded YouTube content is loaded when you choose to play it. Review the linked provider privacy notices for details and choices.

## Advertising and optional tracking

CC Circle currently has no advertising-cookie or marketing-analytics integration of its own. We therefore do not offer an “accept all” banner for optional trackers that are not installed. This does not disable essential provider processing when you use maps, sign-in or payments. If optional tracking is added, this notice and the required consent controls must be updated before it is enabled.

## Your controls

Sign out to remove the app’s stored authentication session. Use your browser’s site-data settings to clear session/local storage and cookies, or device settings to reset the app’s local data. Clearing local data does not delete your server account or service records. For questions or account deletion, contact CC Creative Labs at support@cclabs.space.
