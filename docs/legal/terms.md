## Bookings and event teams

Review the professional’s work, service, scope, location, date and prices before requesting or confirming. A request is not a confirmed booking until accepted. Team-event applicants are selected separately and each confirmed professional has their own booking and availability check. Agree deliverables and any special requirements before work begins.

## Prices and payment

Prices are shown in INR. Fixed-price bookings require verified payment after acceptance and before travel or service. Wedding bookings explicitly marked hourly are paid after service: start and end OTP verification records the service time, partial hours round up to the next whole hour with a minimum of one hour, and the agreed hourly service rate is multiplied by those hours. The displayed booking fee is added once. For example, 2 hours 10 minutes bills as 3 hours. A planned duration reserves time and is not the final hourly bill. Review the booking’s own price breakdown; a professional sees their receivable amount, not the customer’s total.

## Service verification and messages

Share the start OTP only when the professional has arrived and you are ready to begin. Share the end OTP only when the service is complete; it finalises the recorded end time and an hourly invoice. Never share login OTPs as service OTPs. Booking chat requires verified payment and closes when service ends, or the booking is cancelled or declined. Keep your own lawful copies of important agreements before chat closes.

Refunds, when approved, are processed through the payment provider. Do not send OTPs or full bank/card credentials to support.
